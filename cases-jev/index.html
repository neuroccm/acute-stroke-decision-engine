Case scenarios — Jev eval

Every case below is real: 24 of TypeSafeStroke's own hand-written scenarios plus 18 systematically generated boundary cases, each scored on IV thrombolysis and/or EVT eligibility. Jev answers a handful of small sub-questions per case; the real deterministic engine substitutes those answers into the case state and computes the verdict. Ground truth is the tree's own answer to the same case. See how decomposition works.

There is no duty of care. This is an educational website.

Clinical decision support derived from the cited guidelines. It does not replace clinical judgement, local protocols, or specialist consultation. Draft pending clinical review.

Sources: 2026 AHA/ASA Acute Ischemic Stroke Guideline · CSBPR Acute Stroke Management 2022 · CSBPR EVT Interim Update 2025 · Thrombosis Canada IVT/EVT Guide.
